# k0sproject Community

Welcome to the k0sproject Community!

k0s is an open, community driven project to make it easy to use Kubernetes for
getting useful work done. This document describes the organizational structure
of the k0sproject Community including the roles, responsibilities and processes
that govern k0s projects and community.

The k0sproject community has regular community meetings. All events are listed
in the [k0sproject meeting calendar], and the [meeting minutes] are public.

[k0sproject meeting calendar]: https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meetings/k0s?view=month
[meeting minutes]: https://notes.cncf.io/s/_5FVgysYK

## Projects

k0sproject is organized into a set of projects. The k0sproject maintainers are
responsible for driving the projects, publishing a roadmap, organizing community
meetings, publishing meeting notes, and reporting on the current status of the
projects.

The main projects are:

- [k0s] - The Kubernetes Distribution
- [k0smotron] - Efficient Management of k0s Clusters with ClusterAPI
- [k0sctl] - A command-line bootstrapping and management tool for k0s
